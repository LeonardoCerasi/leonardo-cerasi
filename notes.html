Personal notes

All my notes are available at notes. Here, my personal (and better curated) notes are collected:

  • Quantum Field Theory

    These notes are mainly based on the books by Peskin & Schroeder and Maggiore: in particular, the treatment of scalar and vector fields follows Maggiore's book, while that of spinor fields follows Peskin & Schroeder's one. The preliminary mathematical analysis of the Lorentz and Poincaré groups has been inspired by the first book of Weinberg's trilogy on QFT, while the exposition of the asymptotic interaction theory has influences from the books of Srednicki and Itzykson & Zuber. Moreover, these notes have been integrated with lectures by Prof. S. Forte (unimi) and Prof. F. Nesti (univaq).

  • General Relativity and Cosmology

    These notes are divided into three parts. The first part on Differential Geometry consists of two chapters on smooth manifolds and (pseudo-)Riemannian manifolds: these are respectively based on the books Introduction to Smooth Manifolds and Introduction to Riemannian Manifolds by Lee. The second part on General Relativity has influences from many authors: Tong and Straumann on geometrodynamics, Maggiore on gravitational waves, Gourgoulhon and Poisson on black holes. Finally, the third part on Cosmology follows Baumann's book. These notes have been integrated with lectures by Prof. F. Nesti (univaq) and Prof. L. Pilo (univaq).
